Permintaan Hapus Akun dan Data

Terakhir Diperbarui: 13 Oktober 2026

Read in English

Halaman ini menjelaskan cara meminta penghapusan akun TikaPOS beserta data terkait. Aplikasi TikaPOS dikembangkan oleh Made Raka.

Hubungi Kami

Email: raka@tikapro.id

WhatsApp: +62 813-2576-3731

Cara Meminta Penghapusan Akun

  1. Kirim email atau pesan WhatsApp ke kontak di atas dengan subjek "Permintaan Hapus Akun TikaPOS".
  2. Kirim permintaan dari email atau nomor telepon yang terdaftar di akun Anda, lalu sertakan informasi berikut.
  3. Kami akan memverifikasi bahwa Anda adalah pemilik akun. Kami mungkin meminta konfirmasi tambahan.
  4. Setelah terverifikasi, kami akan memproses penghapusan dan memberi tahu Anda setelah selesai.

Contoh isi pesan:

Halo, saya ingin menghapus akun TikaPOS saya beserta datanya.

Nama pemilik akun  : ...
Email / No. HP     : ...
Nama usaha         : ...
Alasan (opsional)  : ...

Data yang Dihapus dan Disimpan

DataYang terjadi
Data akun (nama, email, nomor telepon, kata sandi)Dihapus
Data usaha, outlet, dan akun stafDihapus
Produk, kategori, stok, dan data pelangganDihapus
Catatan transaksi dan pembukuanDihapus, kecuali wajib disimpan berdasarkan ketentuan hukum (misalnya perpajakan), hingga paling lama 10 tahun
Log serverTerhapus otomatis paling lama 90 hari
Cadangan (backup) sistemTerhapus otomatis sesuai siklus pencadangan, paling lama 30 hari

Waktu Proses

Kami akan menanggapi permintaan Anda paling lambat 3 x 24 jam. Data akan dihapus paling lambat 30 hari setelah permintaan terverifikasi.

Sebelum Menghapus Akun

  • Ekspor laporan penjualan atau pembukuan yang masih Anda perlukan.
  • Penghapusan bersifat permanen. Data yang sudah dihapus tidak dapat dipulihkan.
  • Menghapus aplikasi dari ponsel tidak menghapus akun Anda.

Account and Data Deletion Request

Last Updated: October 13, 2026

Baca dalam Bahasa Indonesia

This page explains how to request deletion of your TikaPOS account and its associated data. The TikaPOS app is developed by Made Raka.

Contact Us

Email: raka@tikapro.id

WhatsApp: +62 813-2576-3731

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email or WhatsApp message to the contact above with the subject "TikaPOS Account Deletion Request".
  2. Send it from the email address or phone number registered to your account, and include the details below.
  3. We'll verify that you own the account. We may ask for additional confirmation.
  4. Once verified, we'll process the deletion and let you know when it's done.

Example message:

Hi, I would like to delete my TikaPOS account and its data.

Account owner name : ...
Email / Phone      : ...
Business name      : ...
Reason (optional)  : ...

What Is Deleted and What Is Kept

DataWhat happens
Account data (name, email, phone number, password)Deleted
Business, outlet, and staff account dataDeleted
Products, categories, stock, and customer dataDeleted
Transaction and bookkeeping recordsDeleted, unless we are legally required to keep them (for example, for tax purposes), for up to 10 years
Server logsAutomatically deleted within 90 days
System backupsAutomatically deleted on the backup cycle, within 30 days

Processing Time

We'll respond to your request within 3 x 24 hours. Your data will be deleted within 30 days after your request is verified.

Before You Delete Your Account

  • Export any sales or bookkeeping reports you still need.
  • Deletion is permanent. Deleted data can't be recovered.
  • Uninstalling the app from your phone does not delete your account.

For more details, see our Privacy Policy (in Indonesian).
